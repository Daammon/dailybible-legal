Delete your account and data — Lectiodia

This page explains how to delete your data from the Lectiodia app (Google Play, developer [Company legal name — owner to provide]).

In the app

  1. Open Lectiodia and go to Settings.
  2. Without an account (guest): tap Delete my data on this phone. Your language, tradition and progress stored on the phone are erased at once.
  3. With an account (once the app offers sign-in): tap Account → Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

E-mail [privacy e-mail — owner to provide] from your account's address with the subject "Delete account". We will confirm the deletion.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted within 30 days at most: your profile, your tradition, your completed days and progress, your devices registered for reminders, and your account.
  • You can delete only your tradition without deleting the account: in Settings → Tradition, choose "Not sure".
  • Kept: pseudonymous analytics events (no name or e-mail) for up to 14 months, and purchase records that Google Play must keep by law. Subscription data is handled by Google Play.
