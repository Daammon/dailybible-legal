Privacy policy

Draft 0.1 — 20 October 2026. This policy covers the Lectiodia app for Android ("the app"). A data-protection lawyer reviews it before the first public release (FR-PRV-004).

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is [Company legal name — owner to provide] ([CIF — owner to provide]), [Registered address — owner to provide], Spain. Privacy contact: [privacy e-mail — owner to provide].

2. Summary

  • The app shows one Bible passage a day as a film, audio and text. The films are generated with artificial intelligence; the words are the Bible text, unchanged.
  • Your tradition (for example Catholic or Protestant) reveals your religious beliefs. We only record it if you give explicit consent on the tradition screen. You can choose "Not sure" and we record no tradition.
  • We never use your tradition for advertising, never share it with advertising or analytics companies, and never sell your data. The app shows no ads.
  • Analytics, crash reports and marketing measurement run only if you agree.
  • Your data is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

3. Preview version (closed test, from October 2026)

The preview version used in the closed test has no account and no server. It stores your language, your tradition (only with consent) and the date you finished the welcome steps only on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. "Delete my data on this phone" in the app, or uninstalling it, erases it. The app's data is excluded from Android cloud backups.

The Send feedback link opens a web form in your browser. What you write there is sent to us through the form provider (see section 6) only if you submit the form; do not include sensitive information you do not want to share.

5. How long we keep data

  • Guest and account data: while you use the app; deleted within 30 days after you delete your account or after 24 months without use.
  • Analytics events: 14 months, pseudonymous.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Feedback and problem reports: 24 months.

7. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect earlier processing). In the full version you can export your calendar and delete your account in the app. Write to [privacy e-mail — owner to provide]. You can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

8. Children

The app is rated for ages 12 and over. Users under 14 in Spain (or the age of digital consent in their country) need a parent or guardian to consent for them. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children without that consent.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest; access is limited to the people and systems that need it.

10. Changes

We will publish any change here with a new date and tell you in the app before material changes take effect.
